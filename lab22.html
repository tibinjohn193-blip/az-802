What is Shadow Copy (VSS)?
Takes point-in-time snapshots of a volume (e.g. C:\) on a schedule (e.g. 7:00 AM & 12:00 PM) or on-demand (Create Now), letting admins recover accidentally deleted or modified files via Previous Versions.
