What is a Child Domain?
A subdomain created under an existing Parent Domain (e.g., sales.smec.com under smec.com). It shares the same Forest & Schema, gets an automatic two-way transitive trust, and has its own dedicated Domain Controller & AD database. You only enter sales (no .com needed).
